## Made for the way you watch

BeeForTV is a Stalker IPTV player for Android phones, tablets, Android TV and Fire TV. Connect your own compatible account and enjoy an interface designed for both touch controls and your TV remote.

**This is the official APK distribution repository.** It contains downloads, release notes and installation information. The application's source code is not hosted here.

> BeeForTV does not provide channels, movies, series or subscriptions. Use an existing compatible provider account that you are authorized to access.

## A familiar home for your content

| Feature | What you can do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **📺 Live TV** | Browse and search channels, organize favorites and return to recently watched channels. |
| **🎬 Movies & series** | Keep a Watch Later list, continue where you left off and browse seasons and episodes. |
| **👤 Personal profiles** | Save multiple accounts, choose avatars, switch profiles from Home and add optional profile passwords. |
| **📱 Simple setup** | Add an account manually or use BeeForTV's account QR tools to reduce typing. |
| **🔄 Backup & transfer** | Export and import profiles where file access is supported, or transfer them between devices over Wi-Fi. |
| **▶️ Playback your way** | Choose available audio and subtitle tracks, adjust the picture size or open a stream in an installed external player. |
| **📡 Local Live sharing** | Relay a live channel from your phone to a compatible device on the same private network. |

Features and available content depend on your provider, device and stream. Video resolution and codec support depend on the device's playback capabilities.

## Download & install

**Requires Android 7.0 or later, or a compatible Android TV / Fire TV device.**

1. Open [Releases](https://github.com/BeeForTV/BeeForTV/releases) and choose a version.
2. Under **Assets**, download the file ending in **`.apk`**.
3. Open the APK on your Android device. If prompted, allow installation from the app you used to open it.
4. Install BeeForTV, open it and connect your own provider account.

**Installing on a TV with Downloader?** Enter **`5842766`** in Downloader's URL/search field to access the download. Open the downloaded APK and follow the installation prompts. For more help, follow our [Android TV & Fire TV installation guide](https://beefortv.com/android-tv-setup.html).

**Updating an existing installation?** Install the new official APK over the existing app. Do not uninstall or clear app data first; an in-place update is designed to preserve your saved profiles, settings and viewing progress.
